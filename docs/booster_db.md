# Match Masters – Booster & Perk Database

Generated 2026-10-09 (Europe/Vienna). Machine-readable copy in the repo: `app/src/main/assets/booster_db.json` (same data, all fields).

The live session does not tap from this file. `BoosterAutomation` only plans, and it stays unwired. See `docs/booster_automation.md`.

**Legend.** Category = automation class for the bot. Confidence values: confirmed / likely / unknown. `?` = not documented. Costs = blue pieces needed for a full bar in standard PvP (modifiers change it; always read the `n/7` counter on screen).

## Global rules (apply to all)
- Activating a booster or perk does **not** cost a move (Academy #2 [sb85NrREIz8@0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sb85NrREIz8&t=26s); help center Perks Explanation).
- Booster bar charges from **blue** pieces; opponent from **red**. Some boosters/perks have blue exceptions (see rows).
- Perks: 2 chosen before match, each once per match (Perk Reload: per round), buttons bottom-left under the board.
- Timer stops during booster animations in normal modes; **not** in Showdown / Rumble; in Rumble you can't activate at 0 s.
- Solo modes: 'some boosters are rebalanced… slightly different effects in Solo modes' (help center).
- Power-ups: Supreme/Ultimate Legends only; limited-time events; not in All Stars Solo.

## Conflicts between sources

- **Firecracker**: Exact blast shape (5-piece plus) is from the fan wiki only.
- **Crazy Rocket**: No source states whether the explosion position is chosen or random.
- **Laser Beam**: Help center says 'an adjacent row AND a column of your choice' – whether one tap picks both (cross centre) or two separate taps is not 100% stated; the cross-centre single tap is the most consistent reading.
- **Sweep It!**: Cost 6 per help center & wiki; gamerorbis says 7 (low-reliability). A Reddit thread mentions a period where gold/diamond boosters cost 6 then went back to 7 – read the cost from the bar ('n/7').
- **Balloon Blast**: Fan wiki claims you must make a move after activation – contradicted by owner's recording (score rose with no move).
- **High Voltage**: Fan wiki says 'pick a color'; official says 'choose a piece' (the piece defines the color).
- **Mr. Appleberry**: gamerorbis says the player chooses the color – contradicts official text and official channel video.
- **Queen Cobra**: Official text says COLUMNS; two gameplay videos (Teemo, Dab Plays) say 'rows' duplicated to the right – likely loose wording. Trust official (columns) but verify on first use.
- **Doctor Color**: Not stated whether source/target colors are random or chosen.
- **Monkey Joojoo**: Skull lifetime: wiki says they disappear at end of turn; Teemo says removed after every round.
- **Billie Boom**: Bombs persistence: wiki says bombs disappear at end of your turn; Teemo says 'these bombs will stay out there for your opponent'.
- **Jelly SE**: Teemo (official channel, Jan 2025) describes 2 automatic pools + a 3rd you trigger; official Bunny&Piggy guide says the special is usable twice per activation. Count from the platter icons on screen.
- **Paint Bucket**: Only on fan wiki; current Bronze tier per help center is Firecracker / Color Splash / Quack Attack. Possibly renamed to Color Splash (similar effect) – unverified.
- **Teleport**: Unclear whether resulting 4+ matches grant an extra move (not documented).
- **Mini Cobra**: Teemo says 'select a row' – official says column.
- **Mini Wand**: Help center: random color. Teemo (official channel): 'selecting a color tile on the board will eliminate all the other tiles of that color'. Leo's Academy: 'removes a color'.
